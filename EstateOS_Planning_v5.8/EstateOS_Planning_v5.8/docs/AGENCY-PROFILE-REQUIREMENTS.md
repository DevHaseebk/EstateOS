# Agency Profile (pilot-first D-028)

## MVP direction — approved

Keep onboarding **basic** and let agency configure its premium website through template-specific editable content, not a mandatory verification packet. Agency can publish with **zero property records**, once Admin approval/publication condition is met.

## Proposed basic fields (exact requiredness to approve next)

- Agency name, owner name, mobile, WhatsApp, email (D-016/D-017 historically confirmed on registration; see `docs/REGISTRATION-FLOW.md`).
- Business location/service area and short description.
- Agency logo, hero heading/subheading, hero image and contact CTA.
- About, services, contact address, and optional links/socials.
- Template choice, colors, section visibility/reorder; content fields vary by section variant.
- Properties OPTIONAL (zero is valid).

**Do not require office rental proof, government/business documents, verified map coordinates, or a document review checklist for pilot Approve.** D-023 required those historically; D-028 defers them from the MVP. Logo/image are website personalization controls rather than mandatory agency identity evidence for approval. Exact website content readiness validation is OPEN.
